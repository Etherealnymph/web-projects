### English

A practice terminal for A-share investing: quotes, watchlist, simulated orders, positions, trade history and personal cash-flow. It runs offline on built-in snapshots and can connect to a local FastAPI service for live quotes.

### 简体中文

面向 A 股投资的练习终端：行情、自选、模拟下单、持仓、交易记录与个人收支。内置行情快照可直接离线使用，也可以接本地 FastAPI 服务获取真实行情。
