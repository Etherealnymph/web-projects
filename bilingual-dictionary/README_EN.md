## Overview

The dictionary bundles everyday core vocabulary with EE/IC terminology: detailed bilingual senses, inflections and related forms, example sentences, US/UK IPA and pronunciation. Starred entries carry curated film lines or famous quotations with attribution.

## Features

- 1,684 headwords, 4,711 inflected / related forms, 479 domain terms, 48 quotations and US/UK audio.
- Search in English or Chinese; filter by part of speech and by domain (digital, analog, devices, EDA, embedded, power).
- Click a derived form to jump to its family; random lookup for revision.
- Fully offline: open `index.html`; the vocabulary loads from local data shards on demand.
- A data validator and a smoke test keep the shards consistent before publishing.
