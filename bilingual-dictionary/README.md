### English

The dictionary bundles everyday core vocabulary with EE/IC terminology: detailed bilingual senses, inflections and related forms, example sentences, US/UK IPA and pronunciation. Starred entries carry curated film lines or famous quotations with attribution.

### 简体中文

词典把常用核心词汇和电子工程 / IC 专业术语放在一起：详细中英对照释义、词形变化与相关词、例句、美式 / 英式音标与真人发音。星标词条的例句取自电影台词或名人名言，并注明出处。
